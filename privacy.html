Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Summary

Social Slingshot runs on your own computer. The authors of Slingshot do not operate a server for it, do not receive your data, and do not use analytics, advertising or tracking. Everything Slingshot collects is stored locally in a database file that you control.

Data Slingshot accesses

  • TikTok (only after you click Connect TikTok and approve on TikTok's own page): your open ID, display name and username; your follower, following, like and video counts; and your videos' IDs, titles, creation times, share links and view, like, comment and share counts. Scopes requested: user.info.basic, user.info.profile, user.info.stats, video.list.
  • YouTube: public channel and video statistics, read with an API key you provide.
  • Twitch: public channel, stream and category information, read with application credentials you provide.

How it is used

Only to show you your own analytics inside Slingshot: growth over time, best posting times, top-performing videos and similar-size creators. Slingshot does not sell, share or transfer this data, does not use it for advertising, and never posts or interacts on any platform on your behalf.

Where it is stored

In a SQLite database file on the computer where you run Slingshot. TikTok access and refresh tokens are encrypted in that file with a key from your own settings file. Data is sent only between your computer and the platform APIs (YouTube, Twitch, TikTok) over HTTPS.

Sign-in redirect page

Because TikTok requires an HTTPS sign-in address, this website hosts a page at /tiktok/callback/ that immediately forwards TikTok's one-time sign-in code to Slingshot on your own computer (localhost). The page runs entirely in your browser and stores nothing. The code is single-use, expires quickly, and cannot be used without the app's secret, which only exists on your computer. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub's own privacy statement applies to its hosting.

Retention and deletion

Data stays until you delete it. In Slingshot, Disconnect TikTok revokes access with TikTok and deletes the stored tokens; removing an account deletes its history. Deleting the database file deletes everything. You can also revoke access from your TikTok app's settings under security and permissions.

Children

Slingshot is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required by TikTok, YouTube or Twitch in your country).

Changes

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change.
